What Bin Day Is It?

Add your bins once and the app tells you which one goes out next, reminds you the night before, and lets you tick it off from your Lock Screen, Apple Watch or Control Centre.

  • Weekly, fortnightly, every 3 or 4 weeks, or your own dates
  • Reminders the night before, on the day, or at your own times
  • Skip or move a single collection without changing your schedule
  • Widgets, a bin-night Live Activity, a Control Centre toggle and an Apple Watch app
  • VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion and Dark Mode
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.
