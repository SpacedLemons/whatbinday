Common questions

I'm not getting reminders.
Open the iOS Settings app › Notifications › What Bin Day Is It? and make sure Allow Notifications is on. In the app, check the bin's reminders under Bins › your bin.

My collection moved for a bank holiday.
Tap the collection on Home or in Schedule and choose Move to another day. Only that collection moves; your regular schedule stays the same.

How do I add a widget?
Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, then search for What Bin Day Is It?. Lock Screen widgets are added from the Lock Screen's Customise screen.

What is the bin-night Live Activity?
From 7 pm the evening before a collection, your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show which bins still need to go out. Tap It's out on each one; it finishes once they're all out.

How do I use the Apple Watch app?
Install it from the Watch app on your iPhone. It shows your next bin and lets you mark it as out. Your iPhone needs to have the app set up.

How do I start again?
Settings › Reset all removes every bin, reminder and record from the app.
