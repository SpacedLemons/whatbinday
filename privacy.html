Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

What Bin Day Is It? is made by Lucas Stuart West Rogers. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and its widgets.

The short version

The app doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your devices.

What the app stores on your device

  • The bins you add: their names, types, colours, collection schedules and reminder times.
  • Which collections you've marked as out.
  • A short list of upcoming collections, shared with the app's widgets, Live Activity, Control Centre toggle and Apple Watch app so they can show your next bin.

This information is kept in the app's storage on your iPhone or iPad and, if you use the Watch app, sent directly to your paired Apple Watch over Apple's WatchConnectivity. It is never sent to the developer or any server. Deleting the app, or using Settings › Reset all, removes it.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are never sent from a server. You can turn them off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Tips

Tips are optional in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer doesn't receive your name, payment details or any other personal information. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchases.

Feedback emails

If you choose Report a bug or Suggest a feature, the app opens your email app with a draft that includes the app version, iOS version and device model. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. Emails you send are used only to reply to you and improve the app, and are deleted when no longer needed.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
